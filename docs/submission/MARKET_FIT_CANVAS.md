# Untangle — Market Fit Canvas

**Live build:** https://untangle.pallabdas.me · Evidence below is **pre-finale desk research** (sources linked).
Evidence from finale conversations is recorded separately in [CUSTOMER_TEST.md](CUSTOMER_TEST.md).

| Parameter | What we show |
| --- | --- |
| **Target customer** | **The engineering lead who takes over an application during a team transition** in an Indian IT services firm or global capability centre (GCC): the delivery or tech lead who must make a new team productive on a codebase while the people who built it are serving their notice period or moving to another account. |
| **Problem validation** | Developers spend about **58% of their time understanding code** (field study of 78 professionals, [Xia et al., TSE 2018](https://xin-xia.github.io/publication/TSE17.pdf)). Knowledge loss is constant: TCS alone reported **13.3% attrition on 598,056 staff** ([People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/amp/news/recruiting-and-onboarding/tcs-adds-4258-employees-onboarded-10000-freshers-in-q2-52615)), roughly 80,000 departures a year (our calculation), and **90-day notice periods are justified as knowledge-transfer time** ([Analytics India Magazine](https://analyticsindiamag.com/report-notice-periods-in-india-it/)). **65% of popular projects have a truck factor of two or less** ([Avelino et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.06766v1)). AI assistants do not fix it: **65% of developers correct AI work that "missed the original intent"** ([SD Times on Atlassian 2026](https://sdtimes.com/developer-experience/61556/)). |
| **Current alternative** | Recorded KT calls on Teams or Zoom that nobody searches, KT documents and Confluence pages that go stale, shadowing the departing engineer, and asking around on chat. AI code assistants explain *what* code does from today's snapshot, not *why* it changed. **Why they switch:** the departing person's explanation is lost when the notice period ends; Untangle keeps it linked to the exact file, key or dependency, checks it against the code history, and answers new joiners with citations instead of guesses. |
| **Value proposition** | Ask "why is this like this?" and get the decision, the pull request or call that explains it, what it replaced and who to ask, with a refusal instead of a guess when there is no evidence. Hinglish handover calls are transcribed, linked to code and flagged when they **contradict** the repository. Pulse shows where knowledge is about to walk out. Coding agents get the same decision memory over MCP, so AI changes respect past decisions. |
| **Business model** | **The IT services firm or GCC pays** from the delivery or engineering budget. Published pricing ([untangle.pallabdas.me/pricing](https://untangle.pallabdas.me/pricing), excluding 18% GST): **Free** for public repositories; **Team at ₹1,499 per engineer per month**, from 5 engineers; a **Transition Pack at ₹49,999 per application handover** (90 days, up to 25 engineers, the first 14 days a free pilot, then Team); **Enterprise** at a custom price, self-hosted. Private repositories roll out with pilot customers in Q1 2027. The Transition Pack is the first sale: it is paid from the transition budget, so it needs no seat procurement, and it converts into Team seats. Anchor: the closest competitor charges **$29 per user per month** ([Unblocked](https://getunblocked.com/pricing/index.md)); ₹1,499 is about 40% less. The prices are still a hypothesis to test in the customer conversations. |
| **Market opportunity** | India has **2,117 GCCs employing 2.36 million people** ([Zinnov–Nasscom 2026](https://zinnov.com/centers-of-excellence/zinnov-nasscom-india-gcc-landscape-2026-report/)), plus the IT services majors. Illustration (our calculation): **1% of GCC staff at ₹1,499/month ≈ 23,600 seats ≈ ₹42 crore a year**, before services firms or transitions. Globally, **80% of dependencies go un-upgraded for over a year** while a fix exists 95% of the time ([Sonatype](https://www.sonatype.com/state-of-the-software-supply-chain/2024/introduction)), a second market for "why is this pinned?". |
| **Competitive landscape** | **Unblocked** ($29/user): cited answers from code, PRs, Slack and meetings; no documented refusal or decision history. **DeepWiki, Google Code Wiki**: generated wikis of what code does today; users report confidently wrong pages. **CodeScene**: statistical bus-factor risk, not rationale. **GitHub Copilot Memory**: stores repository facts for the assistant and deletes unused ones after 28 days; no decision history. **Swimm**: moved to paid legacy modernisation. **Where we differ:** answers refuse without evidence; decisions are time-versioned with supersession; handover calls are checked against code (confirm or contradict); Hinglish speech; a shared Neo4j decision memory for both people and coding agents. |
| **First 100 users** | **1. Open-source maintainers (30):** import 50 popular repositories with long histories, send each maintainer a link answering a real "why" question about their code. **2. Agent users (40):** list the MCP server in the Claude and Cursor MCP directories and post a "give your agent decision memory" walkthrough on Dev.to, Reddit r/ExperiencedDevs and Hacker News. **3. Transition teams (30):** five pilot teams from our network in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and NCR GCCs, each running one real handover through Untangle; LinkedIn outreach to delivery leads named on transition announcements. |
