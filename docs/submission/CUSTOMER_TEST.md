# Untangle — 3 Customer Test

Talk to at least three real people from the target segment during the finale (phone, video or in person). Mentors
and guests count only if they belong to the segment. Fill in one section per person straight after the call.
Keep **pre-finale research** (labelled below) separate from **finale evidence**.

## Who counts

The primary segment from the Market Fit Canvas: **an engineer or lead who has taken over, or handed over, an
application during a team transition** (IT services firm, GCC, or any team with a recent handover). Good
fallbacks within the segment: a new joiner in their first six months on an existing codebase; an engineer who
uses Claude Code or Cursor daily on a codebase they did not write (segment 2).

Screening question: *"In the last year, have you taken over code someone else wrote, or handed yours over?"* If no,
the conversation does not count.

## Conversation guide (15 minutes)

Ask about their past, not our product, until the last five minutes.

1. Tell me about the last time you took over or handed over code. What was it, and how long did it take?
2. What did you need to know that was not in the code? Where did you find it, and what did you never find?
3. How was knowledge transferred: calls, documents, shadowing? Were calls recorded? In which language? Did anyone
   go back to them?
4. What went wrong afterwards because a reason was missing? (Ask for one concrete incident.)
5. Do you use AI coding tools on that codebase? Have they changed something they should not have?
6. Who pays for developer tools in your organisation, and what does a tool like this cost there today?
7. *(Last five minutes)* Show Untangle: Ask on a real repository, a handover call with a contradiction, and the
   Agents page. What would you use? What is missing? Would you try it on your next transition?
