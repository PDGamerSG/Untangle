# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/PDGamerSG/Untangle/security/advisories/new). Do not open a public
issue for a vulnerability. Include the affected URL or file, the steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed.

We aim to acknowledge reports within three working days and to share a fix or mitigation plan within fourteen days.

## Scope

- https://untangle.pallabdas.me and https://untangle-code.vercel.app
- The code in this repository: the API, web application, landing page, Cypher queries and workflows

Please do not run automated scanners that send high request volumes, do not access other users' data, and do not
upload recordings of people who have not agreed to it. Billed endpoints are rate limited per account.

## What we protect

Accounts and sessions, uploaded handover calls, and the shared repository graph. The most recent review is recorded
in [docs/security/SECURITY_AUDIT.md](docs/security/SECURITY_AUDIT.md).
